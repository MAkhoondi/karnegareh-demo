About me

See the world through my lens

I started photography at 17 with my father's old camera. I have now visited more than 40 countries, and my work has appeared in National Geographic, BBC Wildlife and international exhibitions.

To me, photography means patience; sometimes I wait for days in cold and rain to capture a single frame. My goal is to inspire people to protect nature through my photographs.
