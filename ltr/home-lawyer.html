About me

Justice is your right

I hold a PhD in Private Law from Shahid Beheshti University and have been a member of the Central Bar Association since 2010. Over the years I have had the privilege of defending my clients' rights in more than 1,200 cases.

Alongside my practice I teach at university and have written two books on contract law. I believe a client should always know what stage their case is at.
