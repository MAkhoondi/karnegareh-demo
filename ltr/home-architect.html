About the studio

We believe good architecture makes life calmer. Every project is a conversation between light, materials, climate and the people who will live in it for years.

Parsa Arian

Founder & Principal Architect

Holds a master's degree in architecture from the University of Tehran and Politecnico di Milano. Before founding the studio in 2015 he worked at architecture firms in Italy and Germany.

Today the studio's team of 12 has designed more than 70 projects across Iran.
