Parsa Arian

Founder & Principal Architect

Holds a master's degree in architecture from the University of Tehran and Politecnico di Milano. Before founding the studio in 2015 he worked at architecture firms in Italy and Germany.

At Arian Studio every design starts with the site: the path of the sun, the prevailing wind, the views and the trees that must stay. The result is buildings that use less energy and grow more beautiful with time.

Design approach

Four principles
in every design

  1. 01

    Daylight

    We set the orientation, window heights and the depth of every overhang by studying the sun’s path through all four seasons.

  2. 02

    Climate and energy

    Natural ventilation, careful insulation and thermal mass keep energy use up to 40% below a typical building.

  3. 03

    Local materials

    We prefer the region’s own brick, stone and timber; they suit the climate better and age more beautifully.

  4. 04

    Everyday life

    Before drawing the first line we learn the daily routine of the family or organization, so the space is built for exactly that life.

Design process

How we work

An average residential project takes about six months from the first meeting to the start of construction. At every stage we review the design with you through models and 3D images.

  1. 1

    Conversation & planning

    Understanding your needs, budget and site.

    2 weeks
  2. 2

    Concept design

    Sketches, study models and 3D images.

    6 weeks
  3. 3

    Design development

    Architectural, structural and MEP drawings.

    8 weeks
  4. 4

    Permits and tender

    Getting approvals and choosing the right contractor.

    6 weeks
  5. 5

    Build & handover

    Design supervision until the building is in full use.

    12–24 months
