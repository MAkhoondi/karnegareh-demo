About the doctor

Dr. Behrad Kamrani

Graduated in medicine and specialised in cardiology at Tehran University of Medical Sciences, with an echocardiography fellowship from Shahid Rajaei Heart Hospital. Member of the Iranian Heart Association and the European Society of Cardiology.

I believe the best treatment is prevention. That is why, alongside treatment, I take the time to educate every patient and answer their questions.
