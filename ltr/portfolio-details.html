Case study

A complete redesign of a creative agency's digital experience

With more than 10 years in business, Navana had an old, slow website that could not show the quality of its work. Our goal was a website that conveys the team's creativity and professionalism to visitors from the very first moment.

The challenge

A bounce rate above 70%, load times of more than 6 seconds and no proper mobile version were costing the agency a large share of potential clients. The in-house team also had no easy way to update the portfolio.

The solution

  • User research with 12 interviews and visitor-behaviour analysis
  • A custom design system with 40+ components in Figma
  • Built with Next.js and static page generation for maximum speed
  • Connected to a content management system for easy updates
  • Subtle, purposeful animation for extra appeal

The result

After launch, load time dropped below 1.2 seconds, the bounce rate fell by 45% and project enquiries through the site doubled in the first three months.
