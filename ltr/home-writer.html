About me

A life among books

Born in 1985 in Rasht. MA in Persian literature from the University of Tehran. I published my first story at 19 in Hamshahri Dastan magazine, and writing has never let me go since.

My work has been translated into English, Turkish and Italian, and alongside writing I run fiction workshops.
