About me

I'm Niloofar, a product and user-experience designer with 7 years of experience at startups and fintech companies. My job is to simplify complexity and build products people enjoy using.

Every design decision I make is based on data and conversations with users. Alongside my work I run monthly events at the Shiraz Designers' Association and mentor new designers.
