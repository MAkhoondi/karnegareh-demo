My story

From grandma's kitchen
to Tokyo

I took my first cooking lesson in my grandmother's kitchen in Tabriz, where the scent of saffron and cinnamon was always in the air. Later I studied at Le Cordon Bleu and spent years working in Michelin-starred restaurants.

Today I'm the head chef of “Sofreh” and I believe Persian cuisine deserves a place at the best tables in the world.
