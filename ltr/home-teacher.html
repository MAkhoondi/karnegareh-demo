About the teacher

Hi, I'm Maryam

I hold an MA in Teaching English as a Foreign Language (TEFL) from Allameh Tabataba'i University and a CELTA from Cambridge. My IELTS score is 8.5 and I have been teaching since 2013.

My teaching is built on conversation, stories and real-life situations, because I believe a language has to be lived, not memorised.
