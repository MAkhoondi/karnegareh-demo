About me

Creativity, storytelling and a little magic

For 10 years I have been creating ideas for Iranian and international brands, from advertising campaigns and motion graphics to visual identities and interactive experiences. For me every project is a story that has to be told well.

Alongside client work I teach at the University of Art, and I love testing bold ideas in my personal projects.
